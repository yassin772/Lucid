# Lucid Product Guardrails

Lucid is a macro understanding layer for retail Forex traders.

It explains the macro backdrop behind currencies and pairs. It does not tell the user what to trade.

## Core Philosophy

Lucid helps users understand:

- what macro regime matters now
- what macro pressure may affect markets
- which currencies have a firmer or softer backdrop
- where pairs show clear macro tension
- when price behavior may align or diverge from the macro backdrop in a future layer

Lucid must remain contextual, structural, explanatory, calm, and premium.

Lucid must not become predictive, signal-driven, trading-oriented, hype-based, or news-feed driven.

## Product Layers

### Macro Regime

Role: identify the dominant macro story.

Inputs: rates, inflation, growth, central banks, market mood, commodities, major macro context.

Outputs: one clear market idea for the Home page.

Must not: become a full macro dashboard or expose every backend detail.

### Macro Pressure

Role: explain rare external pressures that may affect the macro backdrop.

Inputs: filtered narrative headlines, source count, shock taxonomy, reliability rules.

Outputs: calm pressure title, transmission chain, affected currencies.

Must not: become a breaking-news alert, panic banner, or news feed.

### Currency Backdrop

Role: summarize the macro context of each currency.

Inputs: narratives, events, risk environment, driver selection.

Outputs: Supported / Neutral / Weak, headline, reasons, confidence, timeframe, what would change this.

Must not: imply immediate price direction.

### Pair Tension

Role: explain macro asymmetry between two currencies.

Inputs: base/quote currency backdrops, dominant drivers, market mood, relevant catalysts.

Outputs: tension summary, interaction reason, backdrop comparison, what would change this.

Must not: become a trade idea, screener, or recommendation.

### Future Price Alignment

Role: explain whether price behavior is aligned, mixed, diverging, or transitioning relative to the macro backdrop.

Inputs: reliable price data, recent price behavior, simple structure/range context.

Outputs: alignment state and calm explanation.

Must not: become technical analysis, entry confirmation, or a trading signal.

### Learn

Role: make Lucid concepts understandable without interrupting the main flow.

Inputs: product vocabulary and current context.

Outputs: short definitions and examples.

Must not: become a long course or dense documentation surface.

## Banned Or High-Risk Vocabulary

Do not use these in user-facing Lucid copy:

- buy
- sell
- long
- short
- setup
- signal
- trade idea
- opportunity
- edge
- entry
- target
- stop
- stop loss
- take profit
- risk/reward
- conviction
- confirmed trade
- confirmation trade
- price confirmation
- best trade
- A+

Avoid unless there is no safer alternative:

- dominates
- beats
- wins
- strongest setup
- best pair
- confirmed
- watch this pair

Preferred alternatives:

- "has the firmer macro backdrop"
- "shows clearer macro tension"
- "price behavior is aligned with the macro backdrop"
- "price behavior is not fully aligned with the macro backdrop"
- "this view could change if..."

## UI Guardrails

- No red/green trading color system.
- No arrows implying direction.
- No countdowns.
- No urgent alert styling.
- No score, win rate, or probability of success.
- No repeated CTA that makes pair cards feel actionable.
- No combat-style pair scenes.
- No chart replacement visuals that imply price prediction.

Visual emotion should create understanding, not urgency.

## Non-Negotiable Principles

1. Lucid explains; it does not recommend.
2. Macro backdrop is not immediate price direction.
3. Pair tension is not a trade bias.
4. Price alignment is not trade confirmation.
5. Macro pressure is not a panic alert.
6. Confidence measures clarity of context, not probability of success.
7. Events enrich the narrative; they do not replace the macro story.
8. The product must stay calm even when the market is not.
